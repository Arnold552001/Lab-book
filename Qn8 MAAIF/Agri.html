16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). It brings together growers, processors, exporters, buyers and policymakers for three days of business, learning and tasting at Speke Resort Munyonyo.

Visitors can attend expert sessions, meet exhibitors from across the coffee value chain and explore new export markets. Whether you grow, roast, trade or simply love coffee, the Expo is the place to build partnerships that last.
