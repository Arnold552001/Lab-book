Call for Papers

We invite researchers, agronomists, traders and practitioners to submit abstracts on any part of the coffee value chain. Topics include farm productivity, climate resilience, processing, trade, quality and sustainability.

Abstracts must be no longer than 300 words and submitted in English as a Word or PDF document. Each submission should state the author's name, organisation and the session theme it fits best.
