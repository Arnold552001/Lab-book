Welcome to the IT Department at ISBAT University, where students learn to build, secure and manage the technology that Uganda runs on.

Welcome to the Department of Information Technology

Our department prepares students for careers in software development, networking, data and cyber security. Lessons combine theory with regular hands-on lab work, so graduates leave able to solve real problems.

Our lecturers have industry experience and work closely with local employers. Students are supported through projects, internships and mentoring from their first semester to graduation.

Programmes we offer

  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Diploma in Information Technology
  • Certificate in Computer Networking
