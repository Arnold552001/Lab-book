About the Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University trains students in software development, networking, databases and cyber security.

Our mission is to produce graduates who can apply technology to solve practical problems in business, government and community life.

What we value

  • Hands-on learning in well-equipped labs
  • Close links with local employers
  • Support and mentoring for every student
